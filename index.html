Build roadmap

Phase 1 — Digital prototype
Interactive browser interface and concept validation.

Phase 2 — Stationary bench prototype
Off-the-shelf computing board, low-voltage power, and one sensor at a time.

Phase 3 — Enclosure and testing
Modular shell, heat management, stability tests, and documented safety checks.

Phase 4 — Controlled mobility research
Only after stationary tests; no unsupported claims about levitation or transport.

Research and safety note
This project does not demonstrate electromagnetic propulsion, levitation, dimensional travel, or a working physical orb. Those are separate research questions requiring measurable hypotheses, established physics, and controlled experiments. Begin hardware work with a stationary, low-voltage bench prototype.
